### My journey

<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>
I'm a passionate mexican undergrad student with a keen interest in data science, computer science, mathematics, and economics. I'm a firm believer in the power of knowledge to drive innovation and create positive change in the world. My goal is to leverage my skills to tackle complex problems and make a meaningful impact.
</p>
<p>
Currently, pursuing a Bachelor's in Data Science with a full scholarship at 
<a href="https://www.itam.mx/" rel="external nofollow noopener" target="_blank">ITAM</a>. 
</p>
<p>

My passion for applied mathematics and programming led me to an incredible opportunity at ITAM, where I secured a full scholarship to pursue a Bachelor's in Data Science.
<p>
</p>
<div>
